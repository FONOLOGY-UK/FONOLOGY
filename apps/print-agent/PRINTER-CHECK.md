# Printer check — what to press, what to photograph

**For whoever is in the shop.** You do not need to know anything technical. There
are five short tests. Each one prints a slip of paper. Take a photo of each slip
and send them all back.

It takes about ten minutes. Nothing here can break anything, and none of it
touches a real sale.

If something goes wrong at any point, **that is a useful result** — photograph
whatever came out (or write down that nothing came out) and carry on to the next
test.

---

## Before you start

1. Both printers switched on:
   - the **receipt printer** (the wide one that prints till receipts)
   - the **label printer** (the small Brother one that prints sticky labels)
2. Paper in the receipt printer, and a roll in the label printer.
3. Have the **barcode scanner** to hand for tests 4 and 5.
4. On the shop computer, open the dashboard and go to **Printers** in the
   left-hand menu.

You should see two rows near the top — "Receipt printer" and "Label printer".

> **If either row says "No answer from the till PC"**, stop here and tell us.
> The rest of the tests will not print anything.

---

## Test 1 — Paper width

Press **Print this test** under "1. Paper width".

A slip comes out with **two solid black bars**, a row of numbers, and a line of
dashes with an arrow.

**What to check:** each of those long lines must be **one single line** that
reaches the right-hand edge of the paper. The dashed line ends with `80mm>|` and
that marker must sit right at the edge.

❌ **Something is wrong if** any of those lines wraps around onto a second short
line underneath.

📷 **Photograph the whole slip.**

---

## Test 2 — The cutter

Press **Print this test** under "2. The cutter".

**What to check:** you should end up with **two separate pieces of paper**. The
first one ends with `>>> THE CUT BELONGS BELOW THIS LINE`. The second one starts
with `>>> THIS IS THE SECOND SLIP`.

❌ **Something is wrong if** you get one long strip instead of two pieces, or if
any of the writing has been sliced through the middle.

📷 **Photograph both pieces, side by side.**

---

## Test 3 — Characters

Press **Print this test** under "3. Characters".

**What to check**, line by line:

| The slip says                | It must look like                                               |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pound sign                   | `£12.34` — a real **£**, not a box, a `?`, or some other symbol |
| `Zoé Müller Anaïs café`      | the little marks above the letters are all there                |
| `Łukasz Woźniak Šárka Nuñez` | same — all the marks present                                    |
| `Søren Bjørk`                | **this one should print as `S?ren Bj?rk`**                      |

That last line is **meant** to come out with question marks. It is there to prove
the test is working. Do not report it as a fault. (But if it _does_ print with the
slashes through the o's, tell us — that is good news.)

📷 **Photograph the slip.** The pound sign is the important part.

---

## Test 4 — Barcode on a receipt

Under "4. Barcode on a receipt", **choose a product** from the dropdown, then
press **Print this test**.

A slip comes out with a barcode on it and the product's name above it.

**Now scan that barcode** with the shop scanner, into the till's search box.

**What to check:** the till must find **the same product** whose name is printed
on the slip.

Write down which of these happened:

- ✅ It found the right product.
- ❌ It found a **different** product.
- ❌ It found **nothing**.
- ❌ The scanner **would not read it at all** (no beep).

📷 **Photograph the slip** and tell us which of the four it was.

---

## Test 5 — Label printer

Under "5. Label printer", leave the same product selected and press
**Print this test**.

A sticky label comes out with a **box drawn around the edge**, a **small solid
square in each of the four corners**, and a barcode.

**What to check:**

1. All **four corner squares** are on the label. If one is missing or cut off,
   the label is not lined up.
2. The box has **all four sides**. A box missing its right-hand edge means the
   roll is narrower than we think.
3. **Scan the barcode**, same as test 4 — it must find the same product.

📷 **Photograph the label.** Lay it flat and get the whole thing in frame,
including all four corners.

---

## When you are finished

Send back:

- the photos from all five tests (six photos — test 2 has two slips)
- for tests 4 and 5, which of the four scanner results you got
- anything that came out looking odd, even if it is not in the list above

**Keep the printed slips** for a few days in case we need another look at them.

---

## If nothing prints at all

1. Check the printer is on and has paper.
2. On the **Printers** screen, look at the two rows at the top. If one says
   "Not working" or "Needs a look", the message next to it usually says why
   (out of paper, cover open, and so on).
3. If a job is stuck, the bottom of that screen shows anything waiting. A row
   saying **"This did not print"** has a **Try again** button.
4. If the screen says **"Asleep — shop is closed"**, that is normal outside
   opening hours and not a fault.

Still nothing? Tell us what the two rows at the top of the Printers screen say.
That is the single most useful thing you can send us.
